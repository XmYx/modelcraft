# User research: what 3D artists complain about and ask for

**Method.** Desk research done on 2026-11-17 using web search, reading user reviews and community threads about Cinema 4D, Maya, ZBrush, 3ds Max, Houdini, Modo, Nomad Sculpt and Blender. Sources: G2 and Capterra/Software Advice reviews; App Store review samples for Nomad (via apptail.io); vendor forums and request boards (Autodesk Community and Autodesk Ideas, Maxon developer forum and Cineversity, ZBrushCentral, SideFX forum, Nomad Sculpt forum, Blender devtalk, Blender's bug tracker and Right-Click Select, BlenderArtists); Polycount; Reddit threads where search surfaced them; news coverage (CG Channel, 80.lv, Digital Production); and changelogs (Nomad change log, Blender's Save Incremental PR) showing what vendors had to fix. Only user reviews, complaints, requests and release notes were read. No Blender source code was read. Nothing was posted and no accounts were created.

**Frequency.** For each item we counted the independent sources or threads we saw that raise it: different sites, or clearly separate threads or reviewers. **High** = 5 or more, **Medium** = 3–4, **Low** = 1–2. The number in brackets is that count. This is a qualitative signal, not a survey. Search results lean toward forums with good SEO (Polycount, ZBrushCentral, Autodesk Community), and few Reddit threads came up directly. Many threads are several years old, so a problem may already be fixed in a current release. Everything below is paraphrased, with at most one short quote per item.

**ModelCraft column.** "Avoided/has" means ModelCraft already covers it. "Planned" means not yet built. "Rejected" means a deliberate no. Each is followed by a short reason.

## What we build first

Items 5, 7 (hotkeys part) and 10 (panel part) and the first half of 3 and 4 landed on 2026-11-17 (see the ModelCraft column). The list stays for the record; [gaps.md](gaps.md) holds what remains.


Ranked by how much each missing item costs a working professional today: how often it blocks shipping a production asset, how often it came up, and whether it causes data loss or rework.

1. **Retopology (quad remesh plus manual surface-snapped tools).** Without it, a 2M-triangle sculpt can't reach a game or animation pipeline. It was the top request across sculpting apps and the most-cited time sink.
2. **UV unwrap and packing.** Every textured production asset needs UVs, and artists currently pay for outside packers.
3. **Loop/ring selection, plus bridge and fill.** These are the base of everyday poly modeling, and many tools depend on selecting loops quickly.
4. **Snapping, units and measure.** Precision and correct real-world scale fix the scale/unit annoyance (#8) and make glTF exports land at the right size in engines.
5. **Unsaved-changes prompt, Open Recent and incremental save.** These are cheap to build and visible every day. They finish the data-safety story that the journal started.
6. **Booleans as build-history nodes.** A hard-surface staple. Making them live and repairable addresses the "booleans break topology" complaint directly.
7. **Customizable hotkeys with conflict detection, then radial menus.** Every action is already a named command, so rebinding is mostly UI work. It lowers the cost of switching from Maya, Blender or ZBrush.
8. **Reference images as scene objects.** Requested again and again in sculpting communities, and needed to start almost any likeness or product model.
9. **Dynamic topology / voxel remesh, then multires with sculpt layers.** This turns fast sculpting into a full concept-to-detail workflow. Undo must be crash-safe from day one, since Nomad's changelog shows how buggy dyntopo undo can be.
10. **Undo history panel with persistent history, plus a separate view-undo.** This is built on the existing journal. It fixes ZBrush's silently trimmed history and Blender's camera-undo complaints.

Next after these: vertex paint, mirror modeling, multi-segment bevel, a GPU-vendor test matrix with a safe-mode renderer, and USD export.
